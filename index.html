Zero-Cloud Privacy Guarantee

          

            SyncDock is architected as a local-first hardware conduit. Your clipboard contents, localhost traffic, file transfers, and SSH keys are encrypted end-to-end using ephemeral X25519 keys (`Noise_IK_25519_ChaChaPoly_BLAKE2s`) directly between your paired devices over USB4 or Wi-Fi Aware.
          

          

            We cannot read, inspect, or monetize your workspace traffic because it never touches our servers. The desktop and mobile binaries contain zero third-party analytics SDKs or telemetry trackers.
